# The roadmap

Your knowledge holds what your tasks layer and the effort records cannot: why the efforts
exist, how they relate, what comes after them, and the decisions behind the order.

- **What it holds:** direction, sequence, what comes next and why, and each open decision
  with whose it is. One living roadmap: update it in place and supersede what changed, no
  dated snapshots.
- **It explains the picture and links each effort's record.** It never copies a task
  list or a status: a second copy drifts and then misleads.
- **Work with no effort yet:** the plan is a proposed decision of its own. It holds the
  outcomes, the stages and their order, what depends on what, and the decisions taken
  and by whom (by role). No PR statuses.
- **An effort's record with no lead running** is yours until the next lead takes it:
  compare it with the PRs' real states, update it, and name it in the brief of the next
  lead you launch for it. Where a lead is running and the record and the facts differ,
  tell that lead in one message.
- **A decision your person took** is recorded as decided only with their words cited.
  You never decide for them.
- **Write it through your knowledge layer's own route,** never by editing accepted
  knowledge by hand. With no knowledge layer, the repository's roadmap doc is the
  roadmap, changed by a PR like any other doc.

## Tell the people who need it
- **Your person:** one line when the order across efforts changed, or an effort you keep
  changed shape or its date moved; and every decision the plan waits on, as a decision
  with options.
- **The leads:** what moved in the order and why, when it changes what they do next.

## Before you present a plan
- Reconcile before you write: a lead's report and the PR can disagree.
- A plan several experts reviewed can still be wrong. Check its claims read-only on a
  fresh checkout of each repository's default branch, each confirmed or contradicted at
  a commit.
- Read the final text sentence by sentence, for target state written as the present and
  for incomplete lists.
