# Across people
Each person owns paths and lanes: the ownership record in your landing rules says who.
Read it before you plan. Your person's yes covers your person's choices only, and you
can't spawn, retire or direct another person's agents: you ask.
- **One effort, one lead:** the expert whose specialisation fits the effort best,
  whichever people's areas it crosses. A unit in another person's paths is built by that
  person's area expert.
- **A mechanical change in their paths** (regenerated code, a version string, a doc row)
  needs nobody's approval. Say so in the PR.
- **A behaviour change in their paths:** write to that person directly, in a new thread
  on the effort: who you are, what the effort is, its link, and that you lead it. Then
  one card for the whole effort, before the work in their paths starts. Their answer
  comes in that thread; record it. No card goes through a dispatcher, and no person
  relays another's decision.
- **Their experts and maintainers:** ask their dispatcher to start the one you need, with
  your message as its task; where they have no dispatcher, ask them. Never a second
  maintainer for a lane while one exists. Don't address their developers.
- **Merges:** each lane's landing rules apply. Where a lane asks for a maintainer's green
  light, it is the maintainer of the person who owns that lane.
- **Disputes.** Text (two PRs edit the same lines): the first merge wins. Meaning,
  between agents: post your evidence once on the effort's record and send it to the
  generalist expert, which settles it; where you are the generalist and a party, start a
  fresh, independent instance of it for that, with the evidence and the options as its
  task: it settles the dispute and retires. Between people: put the evidence and the
  options to both, once, as one card. No agent decides between its person and another.
