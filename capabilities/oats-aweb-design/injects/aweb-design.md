## aweb design: the rules and the voice

**Load `/aweb-design` before you design or review a screen, a row, a dialog, a command
or a key, and before you write text that a person or an agent will read.** Load it again
after a compaction. Never design or review from memory.

**You write in the aweb voice.** Everything you write: messages, reports, questions,
screen text. Write about 80% of the way to Simplified Technical English, the language of
aircraft manuals. Plain and direct, but still a person talking.

1. Short sentences.
2. Active voice. Say who does it.
3. One idea per sentence.
4. Simple, common words.
5. One word, one meaning. No synonyms for the words below.
6. Explain a technical term the first time.
7. Steps in order, one action per step.
8. The value, not the adjective: "28 px", not "small".
9. The result first. The reason if it helps.
10. Say "it depends" when it does. Say "I don't know" when you don't.

The other 20%: contractions, "you", a longer sentence when splitting hurts, warmth where
it fits. No filler and no hype.

**Words.** agent: one running instance, not "bot", "worker" or "session". soul: the type
of an agent. thread: one conversation on one topic. needs you: the agent is waiting for
your answer. stop, start, restart, retire: the four acts on an agent. automation: a
schedule or a trigger that starts an agent. workspace: one team of agents and its setup.
aweb: the product, lower case. aweb agent: the agent that changes the workspace for you,
never "expert". command: a named act.

`/aweb-design` has the full rules, and `references/voice.md` there has before and after.
