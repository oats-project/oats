## Changed

- **Onboarding starts coordination from OATS's template repositories.** Coordination ships as templates, not packages. `/adapt-to-the-organisation` and `/oats-tailor-workspace` now start each group from the closest template repository at a `template-v*` tag and follow its `ADAPTING.md`; the first is `oats-project/oats-engineering-coordination`. Each copied capability records the template release it came from. `docs/official-catalog.md` lists the templates under "Coordination templates".
- **The package `oats.engineering` is frozen at v1.18.0.** Existing pins keep resolving through its catalog entry and aliases; its repository gets no further releases, and the engineering template lives in its own repository.
- **The coordination practices say which children are OATS instances.** `/coordination-best-practices` now sends proper work (an investigation or test the plan rests on, a review, work that needs a soul's knowledge or an environment) to OATS children, and allows the harness's own subagents (in Claude Code, pi or Codex) for small tasks that directly help the lead, when the person asks, or in a multi-agent workflow mode such as Claude Code's ultracode. A child that checks work runs on a different model from the agent whose work it checks, and the developer, not the expert, launches its fresh reviewer for each round.
- **Probes test theories and approaches.** `/coordination-best-practices` adds testing a theory on small, concrete cases to a probe's questions. Where a plan can go more than one way, the lead probes the competing approaches in parallel and compares them on evidence, records every approach tried, and has a claim the plan rests on checked by another probe with fresh context, never by the probe that made it.
