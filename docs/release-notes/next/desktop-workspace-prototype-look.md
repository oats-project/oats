## Changed

- **Desktop: the Workspace pages look like the prototype.** ([Capabilities and spawning](../desktop.md#capabilities-and-spawning))
  - **The Dark theme takes Tokyo Night's blues and greys** in the window: backgrounds, raised surfaces, ink, the blue
    accent, green, amber and red. Every text colour still meets WCAG AA; the terminals keep their own colours, and their
    cells their size. Light and Solarized keep their colours.
  - **Souls**: **Grid** | **Table** sits beside the page's title. The first line counts the souls and those with
    agents running ("You have 10 souls, 4 with agents running, …"). Group headings are small capitals with their count.
    The grid has three columns at most; a card is a calmer tile, its name in semibold, what it does on two lines, then
    one meta line, and a refused spawn says so in one quiet line.
  - **Capabilities**: a first line says what a capability is, how many there are and how many are official, and what
    ✓, **core** and **default** mean. The rows sit on the page with a thin rule under each, the ✓ or **by** right after
    the name, **core** and **default** as small pills. **Workspace defaults** stays above **All capabilities**, styled the
    same way.
  - **A soul's page and a capability's page** use the same type and spacing: a smaller title, section titles in small
    capitals, the capability and skill rows as Capabilities' rows, quieter tabs and folds.
  - **The status line shows the page's keys** on these pages: "↑↓ move · Enter open · v table · Esc Home" on Souls,
    and "Esc back" on a soul's or a capability's page.
