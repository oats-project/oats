## Added

- **Home in the Desktop.** A workspace now opens on Home, the first pinned row in the sidebar, which answers what needs you and what you have on one page:
  - **The board.** Agents that need you, each with its message, then the running ones, newest first. A group with nobody in it isn't shown, and stopped agents aren't listed.
  - **The overview.** Agents, Souls, Capabilities, Teams, Machines and Graph, each saying how many you have. Enter opens that part of the Workspace page, and Esc comes back to Home.
  - **Home › Agents.** Every agent, running or stopped, with its soul, machine and state, and who started it.
  - **A new workspace** shows what OATS does in two lines and three keys.

  The palette's **Home** (among its sections) opens Home; it has no key of its own yet. The roster's order doesn't change: urgency is sorted on Home only.

## Changed

- **The sidebar's roster heading reads "Agents"**, and the Workspace page no longer has a pinned row of its own: Home opens it, and Home's row stays selected while it shows. ⌘2 / Ctrl+2 and **View: Workspace overview** still open it.
- **A live agent is "running" on Home.** OATS doesn't report working or idle yet, and the Desktop doesn't guess. There is no "New" group for agents that stopped since you last looked either, because nothing records that yet.

## Removed

- **The packaged sidebar brand image** (`sidebar-48.png`). Nothing has drawn it since the brand mark left the sidebar's foot.
