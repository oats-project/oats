## Changed

- **A reviewer soul keeps its own model under the machine's default launch.** A `souls.launch."*"` entry replaces a soul's model as well as its harness, and the per-soul rule gave a soul its own entry only when its harness differed, so a reviewer soul declaring the developers' harness with another model ran on the developers' model. `/oats-package-pins` (after a sync that adds a package soul) and the getting-started skill (step 8, "Record the harness for every soul") now give such a reviewer its own entry with its model, and check that its `oats spawn --preview --json` shows a `launch.effective` model from a family other than the developers'. With no such model on the machine, the person is told. `/oats-tailor-workspace` carries the same rule for the souls it adds.
