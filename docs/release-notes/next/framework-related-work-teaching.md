## Changed

- **The framework's coordination guidance teaches related work.**
  `oats.coordination-setup`'s `/coordination-best-practices` and
  `/adapt-to-the-organisation`, and `oats.onboarding`'s
  `/oats-tailor-workspace`, say that a lead's children are spawned with
  related work, and that a lead for a related effort, the first lead's
  sibling, gets it too. The flag is `--related-work` from OATS 0.69.23 and
  `--same-work` before, which binds a child only. Guidance text only: no
  floor moves. See [Related work](../desktop-cli-api.md#related-work-oats-instance-related-work).
