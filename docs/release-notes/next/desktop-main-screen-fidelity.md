## Changed

- **The Desktop's main screen now looks like the redesign's prototype**
  (the Desktop UI rework, slice 3d). This changes the look only: no key, no
  action and no contract changes. On an Omarchy computer the default colours
  are still Omarchy's own.
  - **The sidebar:**
    - **WORKSPACES** and **AGENTS** are small capitals headings, and the
      sidebar sits a shade darker than the main area in the Dark theme;
    - each agent is two compact lines: its name, then its harness's mark
      (Claude, Codex or Pi, in grey) and its soul, smaller and muted. The
      machine shows only for an agent on another machine
      ("oats-maintainer · polaris"). This computer's name shows once, at the
      right of the status line;
    - **+ New agent** is at the end of the list of agents. Its key and its
      palette entry are unchanged;
    - "new" is a small pill;
    - **Automations** says "1 failed" in red after its name while a
      schedule's last run failed, instead of a red dot.
  - **Tabs** are small pills, 28px tall in a 44px bar: the number, the name,
    then the status dot. The active one is filled and its number is in the
    accent colour. A tab's × shows on the active tab, a hovered tab and a
    focused tab; Delete and Alt+W still close any tab.
  - **The agent view:**
    - each fact is a strip of its own, with its time at the right;
    - the question is a card: "needs you" and its keys on top
      ("1–2 choose · Enter sends · c context · Esc terminal"), the question,
      then its options as boxed rows, each with its number;
    - in the Dark theme, the terminal sits on the app's own background. Two
      colours were adjusted slightly to keep their contrast: ANSI black and
      the selection. The terminal's text size and spacing are unchanged.
  - **The status line** at the bottom is a quieter 28px key bar: the keys in
    the window's own type, with a small dot between two.
  - **The sheets** (Appearance, Keyboard shortcuts, an agent's mail, the
    leader's help sheet) have the prototype's titles, small uppercase section
    headings and spacing.
