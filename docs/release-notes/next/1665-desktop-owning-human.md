## Added

- The Desktop's instance panel shows an instance's **Owning human** in its
  Lineage section when OATS reports a human its messaging identity is
  attributed to (`attributedHuman` on its status row, local or on a server).
  Instances keep their own names as their labels everywhere, and an instance
  with no human attributed looks as before
  ([An instance's owning human](../desktop.md#an-instances-owning-human)).
